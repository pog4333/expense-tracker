Welcome to Track/Expenses
This app helps you and your partner track every dollar — where it is, where it went, and where it's going.
The core idea: Your money lives in two places at once — accounts (where it physically is) and buckets (what it's earmarked for). The total in your accounts should always equal the total in your buckets.
The three things you'll do most
↕
Add an expense — tap "+ Add" in the top right. Takes about 10 seconds once merchant memory kicks in.
◈
Check the dashboard — see your balances, how much you've spent this month per bucket, and any warnings.
◎
Review reports — monthly breakdown, subscriptions, year-over-year. Desktop-focused.
Accounts
An account is a real bank account or card. Set each one up with its current balance — exactly what your bank app shows today.
Accounts & Buckets page
+
Click + Account → enter name, type, and current balance.
$
The balance you enter is your starting point. Every transaction after this will adjust it automatically.
💳
Credit cards: enter your current balance as a negative number if you owe money (e.g. -450.00). The card balance drops when you swipe, and resets when you pay the bill.
Adjust balance is there for when things don't add up. It requires a reason and creates an audit trail — use it whenever your balance drifts from reality.
Buckets
A bucket is a category of intention — not a bank account, but a mental slot for your money. "Essential expenses", "Fun", "Savings" etc.
Key rule
Sum of all account balances = Sum of all bucket balances
The dashboard shows a red warning if this ever breaks. Use "Adjust balance" to fix it.
Setting up buckets
▣
Default buckets (Essential, Fun, Savings etc.) are already created. Rename them to fit your life.
%
Set Salary allocation rules — when you enter a salary deposit, the app automatically splits it across your buckets by these percentages. Must add to 100%.
◎
Add Savings goals to track progress toward a target (e.g. "Emergency fund — $10,000").
Adding an expense
Tap "+ Add" from anywhere. The form remembers merchants you've used before and fills in the details automatically.
Add expense form
1
Start typing the merchant name — suggestions appear after 2 characters. Tap one to auto-fill category, account, and bucket.
2
Enter the amount — always positive. The app knows it's an expense.
3
Check the date — defaults to today. Change it for backdated entries.
4
Confirm account, bucket, category — auto-filled from merchant memory, but you can change any of them.
5
Save — the account and bucket balances update instantly.
Split transactions: After saving, open the transaction and tap "Split" to divide it across multiple buckets or categories. The amounts must add up exactly to the original.
Adding a salary
Salary is special — it doesn't just credit your account, it also distributes the money across your buckets automatically.
How to enter salary
1
Tap + Add as usual.
2
Enter merchant name (e.g. "Employer"), amount, and the account it was deposited into.
3
Check the "This is a salary deposit" checkbox at the bottom.
4
Save — the account balance goes up by the full amount, and each bucket gets its allocated percentage automatically.
Example: $5,000 salary with 40% Essential, 20% Short Term Savings, 20% Long Term, 15% Fun, 5% Charity → Essential gets $2,000, Short Term gets $1,000, and so on.
Transfers
Use Transfer to move money between accounts or between buckets — without it counting as income or expense.
Three types of transfer
⇄
Between accounts — moving money from savings to checking, for example.
💳
Pay credit card bill — this is how you pay off your card. Money leaves your bank account and zeroes out the card balance.
▣
Between buckets — reallocating money without moving it physically. E.g. moving $200 from Fun to Short Term Savings.
Credit card rule: When you swipe your card, the expense debits your bucket immediately. When you pay the bill, use a credit card transfer — this moves the money from your bank account to the card. Never enter a card payment as an expense.
Refunds
When you return something or request a refund, the app tracks it in two stages — requested and confirmed.
How refunds work
1
Find the original expense in Transactions.
2
Tap Refund → Mark pending — this flags it as awaiting a refund. The money hasn't come back yet.
3
When the money actually arrives in your account, tap Confirm — enter the date and amount received.
4
The app creates an income transaction that credits both the account and the original bucket the money came from.
Pending refunds appear as a warning banner on the dashboard so you never forget to follow up.
Reports
Reports are best on desktop. They give you a full picture of your spending patterns over time.
What's in reports
◎
Monthly breakdown — spending by category, every month.
↻
Subscriptions detected — the app automatically spots recurring charges with a consistent amount.
→
Forecast — estimated next month's spending based on your last 6 months of history.
↑
Year-over-year — compare this month to the same month last year.
↓
Export CSV — downloads a zip with all your data. Use this for your weekly backup.
≡
Audit log — every insert, edit, and delete, with timestamps. Hidden from normal use.
Backup & Restore
Your data lives in Supabase which takes its own daily backups. But you can also download your own copy anytime.
Backup
↓
Go to Backup & Restore → Download backup → saves a .zip with all your transactions, accounts, and buckets as CSV files.
📧
A weekly backup is emailed to you automatically once email is configured.
Restore
↑
Upload a .zip previously downloaded from this app.
⚠
All-or-nothing — if any row in the file fails, nothing is imported. You'll see a clear error message explaining what went wrong.
≡
Every restore attempt is logged in the import history table, whether it succeeded or failed.
